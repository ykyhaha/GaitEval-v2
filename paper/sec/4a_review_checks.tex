\subsection{Corrected Splits and Feature-Quality Checks}
\label{sec:review_checks}

\paragraph{GMFCS participant separation.}
The original partition split visits rather than participants: three patients occurred in both training and test, affecting 14 of 899 test clips. Checking validation as well identifies additional overlap. We recover the released deterministic repair, which assigns each participant to the first partition in which they occur, in training--validation--test order, and moves all their clips together. This produces 1,602 training, 478 validation, and 866 test clips from 94, 84, and 166 participants, respectively. Direct comparison of the released participant maps gives empty intersections for all three partition pairs. Each new index retains its original split and sample index, making the 33 removed test clips traceable rather than silently discarded.

This repair is followed by training-dependent reruns, not solely by deleting overlapping test clips from an already trained SFT evaluation. The archived linear references and 7B summary-SFT are retrained on the repaired training partition. Their released SFT predictions contain exactly the 866 corrected test IDs, with labels agreeing with the original records. Re-parsing the raw generations gives 0.3730 accuracy, 0.2585 macro-F1, and $\kappa=0.0431$, with no invalid responses. The archived balanced LR/SVM macro-F1 values are 0.3952/0.4121; the SFT score remains lower. Supplementary Table~\ref{supp-tab:supp_repair_details} distinguishes the original and corrected experiments. The original and corrected SFT columns use different training and test sets, so their small aggregate difference is not a paired estimate of the effect of leakage.

The five zero-shot motion-summary models have no fitted training stage on this task. Their cached raw outputs can therefore be restricted to the corrected test IDs and re-parsed without retraining a model. The archived corrected macro-F1 values are 0.2122, 0.2216, 0.1634, 0.1266, and 0.1905 for Llama, Qwen, Gemma, GLM, and DeepSeek, respectively. These remain below the corrected stratified-random reference of 0.2498; the largest corrected $\kappa$ is 0.0426. Thus, the repair changes the recorded cells and uncertainty analysis while retaining the finding of limited zero-shot agreement in this protocol. It does not support the earlier, stronger interpretation that every model produces exactly the same severity level.

\paragraph{Evaluation records.}
The split-repair experiment has 866 test clips from 166 participants; the seven-condition GMFCS evaluation has 255 clips from 149 participants and 16 supported feature columns. LR macro-F1 is 0.3952 on the former and 0.5788 on the latter. These values refer to different test populations, not repeated estimates on identical records. Within-experiment comparisons retain their own IDs and feature columns. Verification of the repaired participant maps does not certify the unavailable complete training manifest for the 255-clip evaluation.

\paragraph{PATHOGAIT without valid-frame fraction.}
The released ablation deletes only the named valid-frame-fraction line from the 2,181-clip experiment's feature representation before constructing SFT examples. Its generator asserts that the field was removed from every record and leaves IDs, labels, record order, and the remaining feature text unchanged. We compare the archived original and ablated predictions on identical sets of 2,181 test IDs and true labels, using the same six-label parser and scoring rule. Supplementary Table~\ref{supp-tab:supp_novff} reports the recomputed SFT results; the corresponding archived linear-reference comparisons are documented in the supplementary feature audit. The ablated SFT model reaches 0.6410 accuracy and $\kappa=0.5649$, with no invalid predictions.

Removing the field reduces SFT macro-F1 by 0.0554, while the resulting point estimate remains above the quality-free linear references. This establishes that a dataset-specific mapping can still be learned without that field in the recorded rerun. It does not establish a general advantage: PATHOGAIT has two test participants, the rerun is a single training realization, and the source log records a change of serving/training environment. We do not attribute the entire difference to the removed field. The seven shared conditions independently exclude capture-quality variables; feature-SFT on their 1,440-clip PATHOGAIT test set has not been run.
